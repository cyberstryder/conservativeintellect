Local Man Discovers His Own Shadow Is ‘Woke,’ Demands Sun Be InvestigatedSample

By Chip Hollister September 26, 2026 4 min read
☀

Dayton, Ohio — Local patriot Dale Krebbs, 54, has made a chilling discovery that he says “goes all the way to the top of the sky”: his own shadow, he claims, has gone woke.

“It follows me everywhere,” Krebbs told Conservative Intellect, gesturing at the dark shape attached to his feet. “The grocery store. Church. The DMV. It never leaves. That is textbook surveillance behavior, and frankly, I’m tired of being silenced by something that doesn’t even pay taxes.”

Krebbs first grew suspicious in June, when he noticed the shadow appeared to change size throughout the day — behavior he described as “shapeshifting, which is something only woke things do.” By August, he had compiled a 40-page dossier consisting mostly of photographs of the ground.

“The sun has been shining on everyone equally for billions of years, and frankly that’s suspicious. Equal treatment? In this economy of sunlight?”

At a press conference held in his driveway, Krebbs called on Congress to open a formal investigation into the sun, which he described as “the ringleader.” He presented what he called damning evidence: the sun rises in the east every single day, “like it’s following some kind of agenda.”

“Think about it,” he said, squinting directly into the object of his investigation. “The sun gives light to everyone. Rich, poor, whoever. No means testing. No loyalty oath. That’s basically socialism with extra steps.”

When asked whether he had considered simply going indoors, Krebbs called the question “a classic deep-state deflection” and accused this reporter of “probably being the moon.”

Local optometrist Dr. Priya Nair confirmed Krebbs’ vision is 20/20. “His eyes are perfect,” she said. “It’s the part behind them I can’t help with.”

As of press time, Krebbs was seen arguing with a puddle, which he claims is “the shadow’s liberal cousin.” Congress has not yet responded to his request, though one staffer confirmed the letter was “framed and hung in the break room.”
