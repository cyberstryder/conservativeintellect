Brave Patriot Single-Handedly Defeats Windmill in Fistfight, Declares Area Safe for BirdsSample

By Biff Carrington September 24, 2026 3 min read
✦

Amarillo, Texas — In what witnesses are calling “the most one-sided fight since the war on Christmas,” local patriot Cody Brantley, 38, squared off against a 300-foot wind turbine Tuesday and emerged — by his own account — victorious.

“It looked at me funny,” Brantley explained from the urgent care waiting room, cradling a hand wrapped in enough gauze to mummify a opinion columnist. “Those blades, just spinning around all smug. Generating electricity like it owns the place. Somebody had to stand up for the birds.”

According to onlookers, Brantley climbed the perimeter fence at approximately 2:15 p.m., shouted “this is for the eagles,” and began punching the turbine’s base. The turbine, which generates enough clean energy to power 1,400 homes, did not fight back, which Brantley later cited as proof it “knew it was wrong.”

“People say windmills are good because they don’t pollute. Well, you know what else doesn’t pollute? My truck, because the government won’t let me drive it anymore.”

The bout lasted eleven minutes and ended when Brantley, exhausted, declared a “total knockout” and collapsed into a folding chair he had brought for the occasion. The turbine continued rotating, which supporters of Brantley interpreted as “a respectful bow.”

Ornithologists were unavailable for comment, mostly because they were laughing too hard to hold a phone. The local Audubon Society released a brief statement: “The birds would like everyone to know they were fine.”

Brantley’s GoFundMe for medical expenses, titled “Patriot vs. Big Wind,” has raised $47,000. His separate GoFundMe for “electricity, since I knocked out the turbine” has raised $12.

At press time, Brantley was planning his next bout: a “rematch” with what he described as “a solar panel that disrespected my cousin.” The solar panel could not be reached, as it was busy powering a hospital.
