Ukraine Started the War It Was Invaded In, Internet Concludes; the Invading, Records Show, Was Russia’s

By Chip Hollister November 21, 2026 3 min read
⚔

Kyiv, Ukraine — Hours after Russian missiles struck Kyiv again, killing six people, the internet’s foremost war historians announced a breakthrough finding: the country being struck started it.

“Ukraine started this and could end by brokering peace,” wrote one historian on Reddit’s r/Conservative, in a finding awarded eight times by peers. “It’s democrats that lie about Russia to get America to hate them.”

The finding was not slowed by the historical record, which historians described as “very one-sided, in the sense that it only shows one side doing the invading.” In February 2014, disguised Russian troops seized Crimea. On February 24, 2022, Russia launched a full-scale invasion of Ukraine from the north, east, and south — an opening move the historians reclassified as “Ukraine starting something, eventually, by being there.”

“If Ukraine didn’t want a war, it shouldn’t have been located directly in front of all those Russian missiles. That’s just geography.”

“Look, if Ukraine didn’t want a war, it shouldn’t have been located directly in front of all those Russian missiles,” said Dale Krebbs, 54, a local man who has followed the conflict exclusively through comment sections. “That’s just geography. And geography is destiny, or something.”

The historians also predicted that President Trump’s friendship with Vladimir Putin “will likely lead to a healthy alliance soon,” a forecast issued underneath a headline about Russian missiles killing six people in Kyiv hours after a peace declaration.

At press time, the historians had moved on to their next case: determining which Ukrainian bridge provoked the missiles that damaged it.

Actually True

Russia started the war, twice over. Britannica dates the Russia-Ukraine War to February 2014, when disguised Russian troops covertly invaded Crimea, which Russia then annexed; Russian and proxy forces seized territory in the Donbas that April. On February 24, 2022, Russia launched a full-scale invasion of Ukraine — Putin announced it in a televised address, and Russian air strikes and ground forces attacked from Belarus, Crimea, and the Donbas (Congressional Research Service: Russia had mobilized an estimated 150,000–190,000 personnel for the attack).

The world said so at the time, on the record. On March 2, 2022, the UN General Assembly adopted Resolution ES-11/1, “Aggression against Ukraine,” by 141 votes to 5 (35 abstentions), deploring Russia’s invasion and demanding Russia “immediately, completely, and unconditionally withdraw all of its military forces” from Ukraine.

Ukraine did not invade Russia, in 2014 or 2022. In both openings, Russian forces crossed into / operated inside Ukrainian territory; Ukraine’s war has been fought overwhelmingly on its own soil, defending against the invasion the comment says it started.

Sources: Britannica, “Russia-Ukraine War”; UN General Assembly press release GA/12407 (Mar 2, 2022); Congressional Research Service, IN11872 (Mar 7, 2022).
