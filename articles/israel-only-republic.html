Israel Is the ONLY Republic in the Region, Internet Announces, Facts Not Fiction; Lebanon, Which Borders Israel and Is Literally Named the Lebanese Republic, Asks What It Is Then

By Chip Hollister November 24, 2026 3 min read
⚑

Watch Chip Hollister tell it:

JERUSALEM — Israel is the only republic in its region, the internet announced Monday, closing the matter with the words “Facts not fiction,” a phrase that turned out to be the only part of the post that could not survive a map.

The claim, posted under a thread about a 2028 primary poll, held that Israel is a parliamentary republic — true — and that it is “The ONLY Republic, non-Monarchy/Non-Religious based government in the region” — a sentence that requires deleting several countries, starting with the one directly on Israel's northern border. Lebanon, which shares that border, is a parliamentary democratic republic. Its formal name is the Lebanese Republic. It has a president, a parliament, and no king, and it has been introducing itself this way since independence in 1943.

“We checked the name twice,” said Dale Fennick, 58, a fictional local man appointed to read atlases aloud in emergencies. “It says Republic right on the country. That's usually the tell.”

The deletions do not stop at the border. Egypt, on Israel's southwest border, is a republic with a president, not a monarch. Turkey, in the same wider region, has been a republic since October 29, 1923, and a purely secular one since 1928, when it removed Islam as the state religion from its constitution — a hundred years of secular republicanism that apparently still has not reached the thread. Cyprus, just off the Levant coast, was established in 1960 as an independent republic with a presidential system, per its own government's description. Iraq is a federal parliamentary republic. At some point the word “only” stopped doing any work at all and was simply standing there, in capital letters, for morale.

Defenders of the claim were offered the usual escape hatch — that Lebanon's offices are apportioned by religion under its confessional system — and were reminded that this does not rescue the sentence, because Turkey and Cyprus are still standing right there, and because “only republic” was the claim, not “only republic whose seating chart I approve of.”

At press time, the region's other republics had agreed to take turns being nonexistent, so the original post could remain technically undisturbed, and were drawing lots to see which of them had to tell Lebanon it was a monarchy now.

Actually True

Lebanon — the country on Israel's northern border — is a parliamentary democratic republic. The CIA World Factbook lists Lebanon's government type as “parliamentary democratic republic,” its conventional long form as “Lebanese Republic,” and Israel is bordered by Lebanon to the north. Lebanon is not a monarchy.

Turkey is a secular republic, and has been for a century. Britannica: Turkey was declared a republic on October 29, 1923; the clause retaining Islam as the state religion was removed in April 1928, and “Turkey became a purely secular republic.” Its current constitution likewise describes it as a democratic, secular republic. That alone falsifies “ONLY … Non-Religious based government in the region.”

Cyprus is also a republic in the eastern Mediterranean / Levant area. The Republic of Cyprus's own government site: “The Republic of Cyprus was established in 1960 as an independent and sovereign Republic with a presidential system of government.”

More republics in the wider region: the CIA World Factbook government-type listings classify Egypt as a presidential republic and Iraq as a federal parliamentary republic; standard republic listings also include Tunisia and Algeria as republics. Monarchies in the region (Jordan, Saudi Arabia, the Gulf states) are real — they are just not the only other form of government there.

What is true in the original post: Israel is a parliamentary republic, and it is the region's only Jewish-majority state and its only state with that specific parliamentary system combined with its specific character. That narrower claim was available. “The ONLY Republic … in the region. Facts not fiction” was not it.
