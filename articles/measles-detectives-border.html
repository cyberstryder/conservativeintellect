America Loses Measles-Free Status After 26 Years; Internet Detectives Trace Outbreak to Tens of Millions of Migrants, 18 of Whom Had Measles

By Biff Carrington November 18, 2026 3 min read
🦠

Washington — The United States officially lost its measles elimination status on Tuesday, ending a 26-year run, and the nation’s foremost online epidemiologists announced within minutes that they had solved the case: it was the migrants.

“Of course, the left will blame RFK and completely ignore the millions of unvetted migrants who entered the country with no or unknown vaccination histories,” wrote one investigator on Reddit’s r/Conservative, in a finding awarded 14 times by peers. “Import the third world, become the third world,” added another, concluding the inquiry.

The investigation was not slowed by the CDC’s case data, which investigators described as “a real buzzkill.” Of the 5,412 Americans who caught measles in 2026, exactly 18 were international visitors, a figure the detectives called “basically tens of millions, if you round with your heart.”

“Basically tens of millions, if you round with your heart.”

“People keep saying the outbreak started in West Texas and spread through communities where parents stopped vaccinating their kids,” said Dale Krebbs, 54, a local man who has read the thread twice. “But have they considered a hundred million newcomers? Checkmate.”

At press time, the detectives had moved on to their next case: determining which border crossing the nation’s kindergarten vaccination rate used to sneak out of the country.

Actually True

PAHO revoked the U.S. elimination status on November 17, 2026, after measles circulated uninterrupted for more than 12 months. The U.S. had held the status since 2000. There were 5,412 cases in 2026 (CDC, as of November 12) — the most since 1991 — and 13 measles-associated deaths in two years, versus three in the previous 25.

96% of this year’s cases were in people who were unvaccinated or whose vaccination status was unknown. Only 18 of the 5,412 cases were international visitors.

The chain of transmission that ended elimination began with the West Texas outbreak in January 2025 and kept spreading through undervaccinated American communities. Kindergarten MMR coverage has slid from 95.2% (2019–20) to 92.4% (2025–26) — below the 95% level needed for community immunity — exemptions hit a record 4.2%, and about 280,000 kindergartners started the year without a completed MMR series on record.

Canada lost its elimination status in 2025 the same way: not at a border, but in the coverage numbers.
