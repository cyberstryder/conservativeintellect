Man Who Fled to Florida to Escape Government Outraged Government Won’t Fill His SinkholeSample

By Chip Hollister September 18, 2026 3 min read
▼

The Villages, Florida — Ron Pruitt, 67, moved to Florida in 2021 for one reason: to get away from government overreach. This week, standing at the edge of the 30-foot sinkhole that swallowed his driveway, his boat, and his gazebo, he had a message for that same government: where the hell are you?

“I moved here so the government would leave me alone,” Pruitt shouted into the void, his voice echoing off what used to be his dock. “And now I need the government, and it’s nowhere to be found. If that isn’t tyranny, I don’t know what is.”

Pruitt, whose truck bears the bumper stickers “TAXATION IS THEFT” and “MY OTHER TRUCK IS ALSO THIS TRUCK,” says he has contacted the county, the state, FEMA, and “a guy at the hardware store who seemed official” demanding immediate sinkhole remediation at public expense.

“The free market should fill the hole. But until the free market gets here with a shovel, the government needs to do its job, which is whatever I need right now.”

When asked how he squares his anti-government philosophy with his demand for a taxpayer-funded hole-filling operation, Pruitt grew thoughtful. “That’s a good question,” he said, after a long pause. “Anyway, the hole is getting bigger.”

County geologist Dr. Maria Santos explained that the sinkhole formed over a collapsed limestone cavity, a known Florida hazard. “We publish maps of this,” she said. “Mr. Pruitt’s house is literally inside a red zone labeled ‘do not build here, the ground is more of a suggestion.’”

Pruitt called the maps “government overreach” but the sinkhole “government underreach,” a distinction he said was “very clear if you think about it for less than a second.”

Neighbors report Pruitt has begun charging admission to view the sinkhole, calling it “the free market in action,” while simultaneously filing a claim arguing the county owes him a new driveway under the Americans with Disabilities Act, “because walking around the hole is hard on my knees.”

At press time, the sinkhole had claimed Pruitt’s mailbox, which he described as “the final straw, because now the government can’t even send me the forms to complain to the government.”
